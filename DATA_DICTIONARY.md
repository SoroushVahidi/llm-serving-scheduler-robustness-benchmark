# Data dictionary and interpretation

`TABLE_SCHEMA.json` enumerates every table, field, observed scalar type, missing count, row count, primary key, source file and SHA-256. CSV is UTF-8 with a header. Empty cells and explicit missing markers remain missing, never zero; false is not missing. Nested lists/maps in ranking tables are JSON-encoded strings. Floating-point decimal values retain Python round-trip precision. Keys identify records within an experiment; use the release version, execution identity and contract in cross-release joins.

## Identifiers and experimental units

`source` is azure_llm_2024, bailian_qwen or burstgpt; `window` is the frozen 200-request window identifier. `region` is one of LOW, PRE_KNEE, KNEE, POST_KNEE, OVERLOAD, HIGH_PRESSURE, an inherited label whose actual factor is `load_factor`. `condition_id` combines source, window and region. `profile` identifies the KV capacity, kv131072 or kv262144. `policy`, `policy_a`, `policy_b`, `p`, `q` identify the four CM-0.1 adaptations. `budget` is tokens per batch; `slots` is effective resident capacity. `synthesis_seed` pins request synthesis; there is no scheduling RNG. `requests_sha256`, `result_sha256`, `journal_sha256`, `admissions_sha256` are byte/content identities as defined by their originating frozen manifest. `reused` marks a C01 pilot result reused byte-for-byte, not another simulation.

`c01-all-cells` has one source/window/region/profile/policy row; `c01-conditions` has one input condition. The ranking and pairwise tables describe the same cells. `c01-window-aggregates` groups windows at fixed policy, metric, region and capacity, with scope/group defining all, stratum or chronological block. `c01-source-capacity-means` averages the six settings and 40 windows per source; it is distinct from source-mean rankings at a fixed setting. `c01-matched-window-differences` averages six within-window SLO-minus-chunked differences. Repeated conditions are dependent; means of window p95 values are not pooled-request quantiles.

## Cell metrics

- `num_total`, `num_completed`, `num_unfinished`, `requests`: request counts. `completion_fraction` = completed / total arrivals.
- `mean_latency`, `p95_latency`: completion minus arrival in simulated seconds for completed requests. `n_latency` gives the population count.
- `mean_ttft`, `p95_ttft`: first output minus arrival in simulated seconds, with `n_ttft` observed requests.
- `mean_tpot`, `p95_tpot`: per-request time per output token after the first output, in seconds, for eligible output lengths greater than one; `n_tpot` gives the eligible population. Read frozen metric code for exact censoring behavior.
- `observed_seconds`: first arrival to final completion or administrative horizon. `busy_device_seconds`: sum of executed batch durations; neither is CPU wall time.
- `token_throughput`: completed output tokens / observed_seconds. `emitted_token_throughput`: all emitted output tokens / observed_seconds. `request_throughput`: completed requests / observed_seconds.
- `arrival_normalized_weighted_goodput` (ANWG): sum of priorities of requests completed within the synthetic deadline divided by priorities of all arrivals; unitless, not tokens per second.
- `prefill_work`, `decode_work`, `prompt_work`: modeled token work. `peak_reserved_kv`, `peak_used_kv`: token-capacity accounting; no physical memory-byte conversion is claimed.

All latency/throughput results are model outputs. Deadlines are synthetic and output-derived in the metric layer, not deployable online SLO inputs. Completion/censoring counts must accompany conditional performance.

## Rankings, comparisons and aggregates

`metric` names an endpoint. `values` maps policies to measurements; `ranks` uses average ranks under exact ties; `top` is the exact best set, `sensitivity_top` is the separately defined near-tie set, and `missing` preserves unavailable policies. The frozen ranking convention must be read with its endpoint direction; see the frozen analyzer for ordering. Higher is better for ANWG, completion and throughput; lower is better for latency, TTFT and TPOT. In pairwise rows, `a` and `b` are policy values, `difference` is raw a-minus-b, `sign` is direction-oriented preference, `margin` is absolute difference divided by the absolute losing value, and `practical` requires the strict declared margin. Zero losing values are unestimable.

A strict practical reversal requires opposite direction-oriented signs and greater than 10% relative advantage at both compared conditions, evaluated as exact rational comparisons of stored binary floats. `axis` distinguishes window, source, load, capacity, metric and source-mean comparisons. `labels` encodes the frozen grouping tuple separated by |; consult analysis_full.py for axis-specific tuple ordering. `reversals`, `eligible`, `total`, `unestimable` are dependent descriptive comparison counts; `frequency` uses the eligible denominator. Ties remain eligible where denominators exist. `tied`, `slo_unique`, `near_changed` summarize best sets. `observed`, `missing`, `minimum`, `maximum`, `mean`, `total` retain aggregation support; `scope`/`group` define the set of windows being summarized.

## STAT01

`scenario_id` (or `sid`) identifies one of 18 synthetic scenarios; `scenario`/`name` supplies its label. `studies` and stopping `n` count completed studies, not endpoint rows. `family` separates original_prespecified and postdiagnosis methods. `method` is the statistical procedure and `endpoint` the recorded assessment. The complete 25-method × 16-endpoint × 18-scenario grid contains 7,200 rows; structural endpoints such as ci_available must not be mistaken for unavailable numerical values. `mean`, `n`, `mcse`, `lower`, `upper`, `halfwidth` describe the endpoint estimate, applicable support and Monte Carlo uncertainty, using the family's declared uncertainty allocation. `stop` describes the original prespecified stopping decision; secondary methods did not redefine it. Consult supplement/STAT01_PROTOCOL.md and STAT01_AMENDMENT.md for every method, endpoint domain, abstention and multiplicity rule. Synthetic arrays, masks, source-batch lineage and stopping looks remain in the canonical archive. Endpoint rows do not add to the 134,000 saved studies.

## Budget and urgency tables

`baseline`, `perturbed`, `absolute_difference`, `relative_difference` compare a metric at budget 32,768 with the selected smaller budget; relative difference uses abs(baseline). `execution_changed`, `substantive`, `eligible`, `strict_practical_reversal`, `tolerant_order_changed` retain separately defined execution and numerical decisions. `terminal_equal`, `normalized_events_equal`, `raw_order_equal`, `different_request_terminal_count`, `distinct_terminal_behaviors` distinguish identity levels; raw event array permutations can coexist with equivalent selected work.

`batches`, `full_budget_batches`, `ready_batches`, `critical_batches`: batch counts. `ready_decode_opportunities`, `ready_opportunities`, `urgent_decode_opportunities`, `critical_opportunities`, `omitted_ready_decode_opportunities`, `omitted_opportunities`: request-by-batch opportunity counts, not distinct requests. `pre_first_output_omissions`, `between_output_omissions`, `max_consecutive_omissions` distinguish omission placement and run length. `work_per_omitted_batch_lower_bound`, `certified_max_omitted_batches`, `certified_max_age_ms` are certificate bounds. `observed_max_age_ms`, `max_anchor_age_s`, `max_observed_batch_duration_s` retain their explicitly named units. `completed_inter_output_gaps`, `gaps_within_0_5s`, `max_inter_output_gap_ms`, `max_post_prefill_first_decode_gap_ms` describe observed service gaps. Episode records use `request_id`, `omitted_batches`, `resumed_batch`, `anchor_age_s`; request IDs are local numeric simulation IDs. Zero critical opportunities is not evidence of an activated urgency mechanism.

The exact operational definitions are in the launch-pinned metrics/referee, analysis_full.py, budget analyze_study.py, and urgency audit_urgency.py, all included. The JSON schema exposes any additional field mechanically; scientific interpretation follows these frozen definitions, not automated type inference alone.


## TIME01 and ABL01 additions (rc3)

There are 16 additional table views (222,397 heterogeneous rows), for 33 views overall. C01-FULL remains 5,760 unique evaluated cells. TIME01 cells has 768 arm rows (384 new variable executions + 384 reused fixed results); ABL01 cells has 2,880 arm rows (1,440 original SLO results + 1,440 derived controls, no new simulations). Results, copied baselines and derived controls retain distinct experiment identities. Paired-differences rows are condition/metric pairs: 4,224 TIME01 and 18,720 ABL01. Rankings, policy comparisons, source means and aggregates are dependent summaries. The schema lists exact primary keys for every view.

TIME01 mode is fixed or composition-v1. Its cell_id identifies the variable execution, shared across the two paired arms; policy remains explicit. ABL01 arm is original_slo or fcfs_waiting_control; in ranking/comparison views original versus ablated identifies the panel with the changed SLO slot. level is window or source_mean; SOURCE_MEAN is a summary marker, not a new window. family separates intervention, cross_source, and cross_condition; context2 is the second source/setting. Never pool their denominators.

TIME01 differences are variable minus fixed; ABL01 differences are control minus original SLO. relative_change divides by abs(fixed) or abs(original), respectively, and is a fraction, not a percentage. mean_original/control and mean_paired_difference equally weight the balanced cells; percentage statements in the manuscript are ratios of aggregate means, not means of these cellwise fractions. Control mean latency is 1.3660 s versus original 0.9891 s: +38.10% relative to original (the inverse original benefit uses 27.59%). Token throughput rises 3.21%; deadline attainment falls 6.85 percentage points, not 6.85%.

All requests finish. Latency/TTFT populations are 200 completed requests; TPOT excludes single-output requests. p95 uses linear interpolation at 0.95*(n-1); averaging cell p95s does not produce a pooled p95. Rates are output tokens or requests per simulated second over first arrival through final completion, including drain. Deadline endpoints are fractions; with uniform priorities and full completion, both additional ABL deadline endpoints equal arrival_normalized_weighted_goodput. The three throughput endpoints have duplicate winner patterns in this design.

Source means equally weight 8 TIME01 or 40 ABL01 windows at fixed setting/capacity/policy. The six-row manuscript timing table additionally averages the two settings. ABL global means weight all 1,440 balanced pairs equally. favorable/adverse/ties use the metric direction and exact saved differences. Lower latency/TTFT/TPOT and higher throughput/deadline/completion are favorable. TPOT differences around 1e-16 s are floating arithmetic noise; six nominal nonzero mean-latency differences are within 1e-12 s. Exact ties/midranks remain the primary convention. Strict practical reversal requires opposite winners with loser-relative advantage strictly greater than 10% in both conditions; zero denominators are unestimable, never zero effects. Rational margin strings preserve exact binary-float decisions.

Telemetry: duration_* are simulated seconds (duration_n counts batches). duration_p50/p95 are within-cell batch quantiles; do not relabel their averages pooled quantiles. composition_* count batch types or executed tokens as named. urgency_ready/critical/selected_critical/omitted and ABL ready/critical/selected_critical/omitted count repeated request-by-batch opportunities, not independent observations. max_age and urgency_max_age are seconds. admission_mean_wait_to_admission and mean_admission_wait are simulated seconds; n_admitted is requests. serialized_order_changed_batches counts raw span-list reorderings with normalized work preserved; same_state_slo_control_work_difference_batches is a diagnostic on saved states, not extra experiments. deadline_met counts completed-before-deadline requests.

TIME01 and ABL01 code, original manifests, inputs and source hashes reside in code/time01, code/abl01 and evidence/time01, evidence/abl01 in the full archive. The table mirrors contain only result views, excluding request-level session/cache fields. The independent review and portable saved-analysis adapter explicitly separate inherited journal checks from fresh byte verification. No statistical confidence intervals, population inference or hardware calibration are implied.
