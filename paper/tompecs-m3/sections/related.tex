\section{Background and Related Comparisons}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Serving mechanisms.}
Orca established iteration-level scheduling for generative inference;
vLLM's PagedAttention addresses KV-memory management; Sarathi-Serve uses
chunked prefill to manage throughput--latency trade-offs
\citep{yu2022orca,kwon2023vllm,agrawal2024sarathi}. These systems motivate
our adaptation lineage, but their hardware, kernels and allocators are
not executed here. The distinction is especially important for SLAI,
whose published scheduling framework includes theory and a richer
practical policy \citep{slai2025}. Our restricted prompt-ordering and
urgency-gate adaptation neither implements that complete policy nor
inherits its guarantees. Prefill/decode disaggregation in DistServe and
dynamic migration in Llumnix illustrate further mechanisms outside the
single-device, nonpreemptive CM-0.1 action space
\citep{zhong2024distserve,sun2024llumnix}.

\paragraph{Workload-sensitive evaluation.}
Papaioannou and Doudali explicitly examine how dataset and inference
scenario affect serving-system evaluation \citep{papaioannou2024workload}.
Thus workload dependence itself is not our novelty. BurstGPT supplies
production-derived traffic \citep{burstgpt2025}; ServeGen studies workload
characterization and generation \citep{servegen2026}. Our experiment
instead freezes existing windows and jointly reports performance under
specified resource and metric axes. It does not evaluate a workload
generator or claim that three sources represent all serving traffic.
Work on trace resampling for parallel schedulers likewise shows why
workload structure matters \citep{zakay2014resampling}. We retain
chronology descriptively rather than borrowing an independence assumption
from a different resampling design.

\paragraph{Simulation and validation.}
Vidur combines profiling and predictive models to estimate serving
performance \citep{vidur2024}. LLMServingSim composes hardware/software
simulation at iteration granularity \citep{llmservingsim2024}. Their
hardware-prediction purpose differs from our fixed-cost comparison
contract. Following the distinction between model verification and
validation \citep{sargent2013}, we verify that schedules conform to
CM-0.1; this does not establish operational agreement with a GPU system.
The useful question here is which comparative statements survive explicit
work, resource and information accounting, and which follow from the
abstraction itself.

\paragraph{Ranking and inference.}
Comparing algorithms across datasets is well established
\citep{demsar2006comparisons}. A rank, a maximizing set, a practical
pairwise reversal and an absolute performance difference answer different
questions. We do not propose a new rank statistic. Likewise, BH, BY and
Holm are established multiple-testing procedures
\citep{benjamini1995,benjamini2001dependency,holm1979}; their error-control
arguments require appropriate component p-values and assumptions.
Section~\ref{sec:inference} tests a specific historical pipeline and
separate comparison procedures, rather than presenting multiplicity
adjustment as a remedy for an unidentified estimand.
