\section*{Code and Data Availability}
The manuscript and versioned analysis source are maintained in the project
repository.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-scheduler-robustness-benchmark}.}
The prepared reproducibility candidate, \texttt{m3-v1.0.0-rc3}, contains
saved C01 and statistical-calibration records, budget and timing sensitivities,
the prompt-ordering ablation, proof materials, provenance, and integrity
checks. TIME01 reuses fixed-duration baselines; ABL01 derives controls from
proven-equivalent C01 executions and adds no simulations. Identical result-table
views are prepared for Hugging Face and Kaggle, with the complete evidence
archive prepared for Zenodo. These deposits are not published and no M3 DOI
or hosted reviewer-access URL is asserted. Saved-evidence analysis
reproduction is distinct from complete campaign reruns, which require
upstream inputs and the documented environment. Original third-party raw
traces are excluded and must be obtained from the attributed providers.
Original software is MIT-licensed; eligible original results and dataset
documentation use CC BY 4.0. Upstream terms and author copyright in the
manuscript are retained.
