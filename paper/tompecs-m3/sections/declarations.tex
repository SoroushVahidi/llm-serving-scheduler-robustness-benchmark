\section*{Code and Data Availability}
Code and reproduction scripts for \texttt{m3-v1.0.0} are available in the
\href{https://github.com/SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-scheduler-robustness-benchmark/releases/tag/m3-v1.0.0}{GitHub release}.
Result tables are distributed on
\href{https://huggingface.co/datasets/SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-common-contract-m3}{Hugging Face},
with an identical-data mirror on
\href{https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/soroushvahidi/llm-serving-common-contract-m3}{Kaggle}.
The complete saved-evidence archive, including provenance and
component-specific licenses, is preserved in
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23608723}{Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23608723)}.
Reproducing analyses of saved evidence is distinct from rerunning the full
campaign, which requires upstream inputs and the documented environment.
Original third-party raw traces are excluded and must be obtained from
the attributed providers. Original software is MIT-licensed; eligible
original results and dataset documentation use CC BY 4.0. Upstream terms
and author copyright in the manuscript are retained.
