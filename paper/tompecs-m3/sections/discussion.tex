\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{What a ranking should communicate.}
A practical recommendation should identify its objective and performance
cost. In this corpus, recommending SLO-gated solely from its mean-latency
wins conceals throughput losses and BurstGPT tail degradation. Conversely,
zero source-mean practical reversals does not justify a window-invariant
recommendation. A useful evaluation reports magnitudes, matched support,
maximizing sets and the aggregation level together. This is a concrete
reporting lesson from the completed comparison, not a new statistical test.

\paragraph{Model adequacy and informative nulls.}
Fixed batch duration, full-context reservation and a generous chunked-work
budget remove hardware-dependent contention. They explain both the
chunked/decode-first equivalence and the inactive urgency gate. These
findings delimit what the model can distinguish; they are not evidence
that the original systems behave alike. The remaining mean/tail/throughput
trade-offs quantify consequences of the admitted schedules under that
abstraction. Native GPU timing, memory allocators, preemption, migration,
prefix reuse and multi-device effects remain outside the model. No model
parameter was retuned after observing the campaign.

\paragraph{Corpus and estimands.}
The sources are released traffic samples, not randomized provider
interventions. Token-count semantics differ, BurstGPT's selected release
omits failed requests, and window selection includes historical pilot
reuse. Earlier outcomes informed the project; only the full-campaign
extension and its analysis were frozen before new full-campaign outcomes.
Chronological order does not establish exchangeability. Synthetic deadlines
and their joint transformation with arrivals limit ANWG's interpretation.
All requests happen to complete here, eliminating censoring in these data,
but the conditional latency definitions would need explicit support in a
censored corpus. The 10\% convention is a frozen practical threshold, not
an application-validated utility cutoff.

\paragraph{Inference and selection.}
STAT01's independent studies support Monte Carlo uncertainty for its
specified generators. This differs from independence of trace windows or
of the overlapping 165 hypotheses. Its post-diagnosis additions are useful
comparisons, but their finite-family multiplicity accounting does not
undo method selection after inspecting earlier records. Precision gaps
and rare interval availability remain visible. Neither simulation
conformance nor this finite scenario set proves general error control.

\paragraph{Reproducibility and research scope.}
Saved inputs, journals, result hashes, independent validation and complete
summary tables provide an internally traceable chain. External reviewer
access and release-specific permissions are separate submission obligations.
Related manuscripts share code and workload lineage; their native probes
or mechanism interventions are not validation of CM-0.1. This paper's
contribution is the whole-window comparison of identified adaptations,
not a claim of independent evidence from every related project.
