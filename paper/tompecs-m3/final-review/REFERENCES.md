# Reference coverage and verification

The reviewed draft has 20 entries. The correction has **24**, adding two directly relevant evaluation/simulation papers and two dataset-requested attributions. This is a targeted coverage review, not an exhaustive serving-system bibliography. DOI identities were compared with publisher-deposited Crossref metadata; new results are saved in [reference-metadata.json](reference-metadata.json) and [DynamoLLM metadata](dynamollm-metadata.json). Four fresh Crossref requests returned 429; those failures are retained rather than labeled successful. Existing M3 metadata and the primary sources below provide the remaining checks. A publisher identity check is distinguished from a full-paper review.

## Verified missing work and changes

1. **Etalon** — Agrawal et al., [original paper, arXiv:2407.07000v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.07000v2). Directly relevant to metric choice, streaming latency, and the limitations of conventional aggregate endpoints. Added a short comparison: M3 analyzes its frozen endpoints and does not measure Etalon's fluidity index or introduce a user-experience metric. Its publication status is represented as the verified preprint, not guessed.
2. **LLMServingSim 2.0** — Cho, Choi, Heo and Park, [original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23036v1), [official ISPASS 2026 program](https://ispass.org/ispass2026/program.php/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISPASS69572.2026.00012). Title, authors, pages 1–14 and publication year verified with fresh deposited metadata. Added comparison to runtime/hardware/disaggregation interaction modeling. Its richer simulator and empirical validation make CM's intentionally different purpose clearer; neither tool is claimed to have validated M3.
3. **DynamoLLM** — Stojkovic et al., [authors' paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00741), [HPCA DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/HPCA61900.2025.00102), verified 1348–1362. Added because the Azure dataset explicitly requests it. This attribution does not claim M3 models energy or reconfiguration.
4. **KVCache Cache in the Wild** — Wang et al., [official ATC 2025 paper/BibTeX](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc25/presentation/wang-jiahao), 465–482. Added because the pinned Bailian/Qwen release requests it. Its cache-reuse results are not evaluated by CM, which omits prefix reuse.

These additions are justified by exact relevance or explicit dataset provenance, not citation counts.

## Considered but not padded into this correction

- [Splitwise](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/splitwise-efficient-generative-llm-inference-using-phase-splitting/) (ISCA 2024) is influential primary evidence for phase-specific resource behavior. It would strengthen an expanded discussion of why constant-duration CM differs from hardware serving. DistServe already establishes the narrower disaggregation boundary used here; omission is not a missing-direct-comparator blocker.
- [GenZ: Demystifying Platform Requirements for Diverse LLM Inference Use Cases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01698) is verified analytical performance-modeling work. Useful if M3 expands hardware-model comparisons, but not necessary to support the bounded present claims.
- [Li, Dai and Peng, Throughput-Optimal Scheduling Algorithms for LLM Inference and AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07347) is relevant queueing theory if a sustainable-throughput/stability discussion is developed. M3 measures drain-inclusive finite-window completion rates, so its scheduler theorems must not be imported. No assertion about a final publication venue is made here.
- A general bootstrap foundation such as Efron's original work may be useful in an expanded statistics discussion; it would not validate the custom screened practical-reversal procedure. The current main-text diagnostic is intentionally concise and already cites the named multiple-testing/concentration procedures.

The most important remaining missing references are **author-controlled related-manuscript identifiers**, not more unrelated serving systems. Do not invent citations or publication statuses for them. The current [overlap audit](OVERLAP_AUDIT.md) supplies the scientific distinctions that a confirmed disclosure must convey.
