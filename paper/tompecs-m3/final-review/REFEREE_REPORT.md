# Final M3 referee and readiness review

Reviewed version: PR #19, `ed24b2e8224ea88aa289b60dea6142beeea5330d`. Review date: 2026-10-22. Corrections are stacked on that exact version. **Verdict: NOT_READY.** Task state: **COMPLETED_SUCCESS** (review and correction package; not submission clearance).

The paper is an unusually traceable, deliberately restricted performance evaluation. It reports unfavorable results, exact equivalences, and null aggregate reversal results without manufacturing significance. Its central numerical findings are supported by saved evidence. Nevertheless, I would recommend **major revision rather than acceptance** as a skeptical ToMPECS referee. The principal scientific weakness is the limited explanatory reach of an execution model that makes several of the nominal scheduler distinctions inactive. There are also unresolved author, overlap, artifact, and venue gates. A successful build and a large cell count do not resolve either category.

This assessment is independent reading and recomputation of saved summaries, not an independent experimental replication. No new experiment, simulator run, Monte Carlo study, Slurm submission, or native-system validation was performed.
