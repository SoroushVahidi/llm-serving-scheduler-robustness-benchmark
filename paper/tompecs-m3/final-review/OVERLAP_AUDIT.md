## Publication-ethics implications and author action

Shared traces, loaders, and terminology are not by themselves redundant publication. Reusing a rejected manuscript for a new venue is not established misconduct. Preprints are distinct from simultaneous peer review. Conversely, new prose, a new title, or extra experiments do not establish sufficient novelty if the same contribution is concurrently submitted or already published.

The author must resolve two different “Performance Evaluation” lines: the old LSSP portability submission and the current headroom manuscript. Provide title, exact uploaded PDF/source hash, submission ID/date, current disposition, and any public DOI/preprint for each, and similarly for SMPT, JSC, FGCS/LLM2026 history, and module work. If another active or published version is substantively overlapping, determine the applicable disclosure or withdrawal path **before submission**. This audit neither determines that misconduct occurred nor grants an exception to a publisher policy.

An author-approved disclosure should explicitly state: reused historical windows/reconstruction and the archived 36/990 result; new CM and calibration evidence; what differs from the adjacent action-headroom and module-attribution studies; which native-system findings are not being claimed as M3 validation; and any unresolved exact BurstGPT overlap. Supply related manuscripts to editors when required by the confirmed venue policy. No such correspondence is authorized or performed here.
