## Assessment

The strongest independent contribution is the *connection* between a contract-specific transition invariant, a sufficient inactivity certificate, and the interpretation of measured preferences after demonstrated redundancies are removed. Neither the inequalities nor the observation that objectives conflict should be advertised as a broad new scheduling theory. The result is a carefully verified model case study; whether its explanatory reach warrants a full journal article remains a substantive novelty question that prose cannot settle.
