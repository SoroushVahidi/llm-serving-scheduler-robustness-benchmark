\section{Scheduler Ranking Portability}
\label{sec:results}

Every subsection below is a result contract: an exact specification of what
will be reported once Pilot-V2 executes (\S\ref{sec:artifact} states current
execution status), reproduced from
\texttt{docs/MANUSCRIPT\_RESULT\_CONTRACT.md}, the project's single source of
truth for contract wording. No subsection below asserts a direction of
finding. Either a high-portability or a low-portability outcome is a
complete, reportable result under this design (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).

\subsection{Discriminability}
\label{sec:discriminability}

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: fraction of non-tied (window, load-region)
conditions per source, per metric, over the full 13-policy / 6-region
Pilot-V2 matrix, with Wilson 95\% confidence intervals per source (method
reused from the Stage-0 diagnostic, \S\ref{sec:stage0}). Historical Stage-0
evidence (3 sources $\times$ 3 regions $\times$ 6-policy subset only) is
reported separately in \S\ref{sec:stage0} and is not substituted for this
result.]}

\subsection{Cross-Source Rankings}
\label{sec:cross-source-rankings}

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: Kendall's tau-b and Spearman's rho between
each pair of the three sources' policy rankings, per metric
(\S\ref{sec:metrics}), per operating region, with block-bootstrap 95\%
confidence intervals over windows. Reported alongside top-1/top-3 overlap
and \texttt{n\_conditions\_excluded\_for\_undefined\_metric} per cell. No
claim of ``stable'' or ``unstable'' rankings is made before this table
exists.]}

\subsection{Pairwise Reversals}
\label{sec:reversals}

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: every (policy A, policy B) pair's reversal
frequency and count of \emph{practically meaningful} reversals only --
defined as exceeding the preregistered 10\% practical-margin threshold with
a confidence interval excluding zero -- reported separately from
microscopic sign changes. No reversal count mixing the two categories is
reported.]}

\subsection{Temporal/OOD Portability}
\label{sec:temporal}

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: three separately labeled comparisons --
within-BurstGPT EARLY/MIDDLE/LATE tau, Azure-2024 own-window calendar-split
tau, and cross-source (provider/domain) tau from
\S\ref{sec:cross-source-rankings} -- never merged into a single
``temporal/domain OOD'' number.]}

\subsection{Load Conditioning}
\label{sec:load-conditioning}

Load-level dependence is a secondary robustness/sensitivity analysis
(\S\ref{sec:rq}), not a headline claim of cross-source rank instability by
itself.

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: tau and reversal frequency across the six
frozen operating regions (\S\ref{sec:calibration}), per source, reported as
a robustness/sensitivity table using this project's own independently
calibrated load protocol -- never LLM 2026's $\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128\}$ grid
or its 60-window set (\S\ref{sec:evidence-boundaries}).]}

\subsection{Metric Conditioning}
\label{sec:metric-conditioning}

\emph{[PENDING PILOT-V2 RESULT: tau between every pair of metrics in the
metric contract (\S\ref{sec:metrics}), per source, reported alongside
whether any exclusion-driven gap from undefined completion-conditioned
metrics is concentrated in one source -- a candidate explanatory factor if
so, reported descriptively, not causally.]}
