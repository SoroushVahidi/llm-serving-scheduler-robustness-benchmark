\paragraph{Data availability.} This study distinguishes four categories of
data, consistent with \S\ref{sec:artifact}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Externally sourced public traces} (BurstGPT, Azure 2024,
    Bailian/Qwen) are governed by their original publishers' release terms
    and license status (\S\ref{sec:provenance}; BurstGPT: CC-BY-4.0) and
    are not redistributed as part of this project's own release; readers
    should obtain them from their original sources.
  \item \textbf{Derived benchmark manifests} -- the frozen window
    identities, provenance, and content hashes (\S\ref{sec:windows}) -- and
  \item \textbf{Generated campaign results} -- the full paired policy
    outcome matrix across windows, regions, and metrics (\S\ref{sec:results})
    -- and
  \item \textbf{Analysis artifacts} -- ranking-portability statistics,
    reversal tables, and reproduction scripts (\S\ref{sec:artifact}) --
  will be archived at a persistent, publicly accessible repository (the
  Hugging Face repository slug \texttt{SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-scheduler-portability})
  upon completion of the study reported here. \textbf{As of this
  manuscript, that artifact is planned but not yet released}
  (\S\ref{sec:artifact}); no data availability claim in this statement
  should be read as an assertion that the release is already live.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Code availability.} The simulator, policy implementations, and
analysis pipeline underlying this study will be released alongside the
data described above, under the same repository, once the study
completes.
