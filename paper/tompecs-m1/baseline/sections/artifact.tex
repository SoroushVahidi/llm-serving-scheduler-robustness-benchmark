\section{Reproducibility and the LSSP Artifact}
\label{sec:artifact}

LSSP is released to support independent reproduction through linked
public artifacts: a GitHub repository
(\url{https://github.com/SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-scheduler-robustness-benchmark})
carrying the simulator, policy implementations, analysis and validation
pipeline, figure/table generation code, manuscript source, and frozen
provenance manifests for every campaign stage; a Hugging Face dataset
(\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/SoroushVahidi/llm-serving-scheduler-portability})
carrying the analysis-ready principal result tables and the reduced RQ6
result with computational provenance (\S\ref{sec:real-system-result}); and a
Zenodo snapshot, DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22306798}{10.5281/zenodo.22306798}.
Upstream traces (\S\ref{sec:provenance}) remain governed by their
publishers' licenses, and several per-cell campaign records are not yet
public; the full public/non-public release boundary is stated under Data
Availability in the Declarations.

The primary comparative outcome (\S\ref{sec:results}) was generated from,
and structurally validated against, the full paired policy-outcome matrix
across windows, regions, and metrics -- including the per-cell mechanism
telemetry (\S\ref{sec:telemetry}) -- prior to any scientific
interpretation. The primary statistical analysis was executed once,
deterministically, against the frozen $9{,}360$-configuration matrix, and
was independently structurally validated before any number in
\S\ref{sec:results} or \S\ref{sec:sample-complexity} was populated. The
full validation record, scientific-freeze hashes, configuration and
workload-manifest hashes, and per-cell seed/provenance fields are committed
to the public repository's artifact documentation rather than reproduced in
the main text.

The public artifacts are therefore sufficient to inspect the released
principal result tables, reduced RQ6 analysis, frozen manifests, code, and
provenance, but they are not a complete public dump of every intermediate
or per-execution record generated by the campaign.
