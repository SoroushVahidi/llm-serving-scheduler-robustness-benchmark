# Scientific readiness boundary

**GO: one CPU CM-0.1 conformance pilot.** Readiness is limited to the declared common model and four project-maintained adaptations. **NO-GO: full original/native-policy claims, full36-record persistence estimate, publication readiness or automatic Job2.**

The reuse audit found no equivalent common-model target cells. Existing results supply frozen workloads, load factors, historical rankings/reversal labels and engineering counterexamples. The new contribution is a reproducible comparison in which policy IDs do not change the executor, cost function, resource rules, permissible information or metrics. Under that model, differences among adaptations arise from their declared scheduling decisions. The two KV profiles additionally isolate a common capacity change while holding policy code and other inputs fixed. Historical-to-new differences combine several harmonization/adaptation changes and do not identify their separate causal effects.

The six region names are **historical labels for frozen arrival compression factors**. They are not a recalibration of the new model's saturation knee. Keeping those arrivals fixed is required for a paired historical comparison; claiming that KNEE remains the new model's knee would be incorrect.

Limitations that survive successful execution:

* First canonical window/source gives only three EARLY windows, not temporal coverage or independent provider replication. Repeated policies/regions are dependent observations of those same requests.
* Full-context KV reservation permits only four/eight residents, despite a64-sequence ceiling. This is a deliberately conservative nonpreemptive contract, not native incremental allocation. Alternative allocation/recompute contracts require new implementation and validation.
* Fixed1ms batch timing is synthetic and insensitive to batch composition. No physical GPU cost or timing validation follows. Raising the shared budget to32,768 admits all frozen prompts but changes the historical execution contract substantially.
* New policy IDs denote scheduling adaptations. Complete SLAI, original paged allocation/preemption and native final-prefill sampling are not represented. Unchanged original algorithms are excluded from comparability claims.
* Historical metric deadlines depend on predictions synthesized around actual output length. Those values remain metric-only, never online policy inputs. This is a historical-score comparison, not a validation of deployable SLOs.
* A50-second drain may leave requests unfinished. Completion/ANWG retain all arrivals; conditional latency support and both completed/emitted token throughputs must remain explicit. No imputation or zero-latency completion is allowed.
*58 genuine stable-ordering controls,14 microscopic sign changes and36 historically supported records are preserved. None receives new statistical significance. Pilot signs/margins are descriptive; full-corpus persistence remains not evaluable.

## Job2 gate

Do not submit Job2 automatically. First obtain the completed Slurm record and exit code,144 independently valid cell journals/results, all input/source fingerprints, no unhandled failure, and a review of truncation, ties, resource saturation and adaptation behavior. Examine whether the conservative reservation/budget leaves enough scheduling variation to motivate expansion; an all-tied outcome is valid and may argue against it. Check paired KV sensitivity and all absolute metrics. Then choose a prospective follow-up with author authorization: full frozen-window coverage, additional contract ablations, or a justified NO-GO. Native/GPU validation is separate and not authorized by this CPU pilot.
