# Prospective analysis recommendation — conditional, before calibration outcomes

The calibration campaign is a falsification/diagnostic exercise, not a license to select whichever method produces more discoveries. This document is written before campaign outcomes. A later result report must distinguish finite-sample arguments, assumption checks and empirical calibration on these18 scenarios.

**For a future experiment with independent bounded workload units:** predeclare the source/population target, all policy/source comparisons, the practical reversal alternative, and the metric's fixed bounds. Treat all arrivals consistently; avoid undefined conditional latency as a primary score unless its target is explicit. The bounded-Hoeffding component tests, two-orientation Bonferroni IUT construction and full-family Holm correction give a conservative defensible route. This controls FWER (hence FDR) under the stated independent bounded-sample assumptions, without requiring comparison independence. Low power is possible and must be addressed through prospective sample-size planning, not outcome-selected families or more permissive post-hoc tests.

For one-sided contrast mean d>0 with width2.1, the conservative rough requirement p≤α/(2m) is n≥(2.1²/(2d²))log(2m/α). This is a deterministic signal/threshold planning calculation, **not a power guarantee**: observed means fluctuate. Here m165 andα.05. Independent bounded cluster averages can be the units when cluster boundaries and independence are defensible; the number of independent clusters, not requests/windows inside them, determines the bound.

**For Gaussian independent blocks:** cluster-mean Student tests followed by the same full-family reversal construction/Holm are exact under their Gaussian/block assumptions. Native workloads do not acquire those assumptions because they pass a small normality test. Unequal source variances are handled separately at each source; overlapping policy comparisons remain dependent but Holm does not require their independence.

**For temporally dependent or selectively missing outcomes:** do not inherit nominal95%/5% claims from historical bootstrap/BH or from one apparently favorable Monte Carlo scenario. Fixed-length grouped resampling is diagnostic here; it is not a universal solution for irregularly spaced, source-related windows. Oracle normal tests are positive controls, not prospective tools when covariance is unknown. Selective missingness requires a defined observed-support estimand or an independently justified observation model; matching windows alone does not identify latent unconditional means.

Use set-valued confidence candidates for tied optimal policies when valid simultaneous dominance tests exist. Deterministic policy-ID choices are implementation conventions, not evidence of unique optima.

Before adopting a procedure: inspect complete calibrated FDR/FWER, coverage, power, availability and Monte Carlo bounds on every relevant scenario, including negatives; justify future sampling units and assumptions independently; freeze the future protocol. Failure of an assumption-valid benchmark triggers investigation of generator/test implementation, not automatic tuning or rerunning a favorable subset. No observed LLM-serving claims or M2 statements are changed by this campaign.
