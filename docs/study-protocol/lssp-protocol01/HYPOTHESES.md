# Falsifiable hypotheses

**DRAFT.** IDs and proposed tests are frozen only after author approval. Numerical margins below are proposals, not retrospectively selected thresholds. Let Y be SLO-compliant requests / all offered requests in a fixed cohort, Δ(d,a,b)=E[Y(d,a)−Y(d,b)], and I(x→y,a,b)=Δ(y,a,b)−Δ(x,a,b). Expectations use the prespecified window population, with equal source weighting only in the separately labeled pooled summary. Proposed practical margin δ=0.05 (five percentage points of offered traffic); proposed interval half-width target h=0.025.

| ID / contribution | Falsifiable proposition and alternative | Necessary experiments / measurements | Analysis and acceptance | Evidence does not justify |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 / C1 | Intended and executed decisions agree for every supported tested state. A single mismatch falsifies conformance for that contract. | Independent reference fixtures; adapter/native/runner reconciliation on all deployments; explicit negative cases | Zero mismatches, all mandatory paths covered, pairwise distinguishability and complete request accounting. Unknown paths stay NOT VERIFIED. | Universal correctness from finite tests; identity with an external algorithm without a pinned semantic mapping |
| H2a / C2 | For a declared transfer edge/policy pair, the advantage changes by less than δ: −δ<I<δ. Alternative: a materially different advantage. | Paired windows across both endpoints, policies, pressures and fresh repetitions | Simultaneous CI entirely inside [−δ,δ] supports practical equivalence of advantage. CI outside that band supports a material interaction; overlap is inconclusive. | Equivalence from a nonsignificant zero-effect test; no change in absolute latency |
| H2b / C2 | Policy preference is concordant across deployments. Alternative: both endpoints support opposite advantages beyond δ. | Same paired panel, including overload and failures | Reversal only if simultaneous endpoint intervals lie beyond opposite ±δ margins. Concordant preference requires the same supported sign; intervals within ±δ imply practical ties. Anything else unresolved. | A required reversal, universal ranking, or prevalence outside the sampled domain |
| H3q / C3 | Increasing prefill contention changes the advantage of decode-sensitive mechanisms in association with decode-gap and queue-delay changes. Null: no reproducible interaction. | Outcome-blind low/high-prefill strata; validated chunk-budget intervention at fixed work/load | Prespecified interaction sign for the intervention, paired CI, and intermediate measurements must agree. | H3c / C3 | Larger collective critical-path share modifies policy advantage in TP2 relative to the corresponding single-GPU/DP2 cases. Null: no added explanatory value. | Rank-synchronized phase/collective profiles, matched work and topology, selected communication-sensitive strata | Out-of-sample gain over pressure/length-only model; controlled topology/collective intervention needed for a causal communication claim | That A100/L40 differences are caused solely by communication; sums of overlapping GPU kernels as elapsed time |
| H3r / C3 | Replica work imbalance changes DP2 policy advantage; balancing routing reduces the imbalance pathway. Null: balancing does not alter it. | Fixed round-robin primary routing versus a separately validated work-aware router on prespecified fixtures | Paired randomized router intervention; observe arrival, queue and service imbalance and its effect interval | A better online router or production routing theorem |
| H3k / C3 | KV headroom changes policy advantage when it changes admission/recomputation behavior. Null: no such interaction within tested headroom. | Validated cache-budget intervention; block allocation, victim/recompute and admission logs | Only run where all policies support declared KV behavior; verify the intervention changes the mediator, then estimate performance contrast | A KV explanation when no pressure event occurred, or native victim rules substituted for SLAI |
| H4 / C4 | A locked transfer rule can certify low regret with useful coverage and net savings. Null: the risk bound, coverage or cost criterion fails. | Unseen temporal blocks and deployment-edge stress test; source choice, target comparator and probe costs | Proposed false-safe risk upper bound ≤5%, regret tolerance δ, coverage lower bound ≥25%, and net savings lower bound >0; all must pass. Otherwise abstention/recalibration remains the recommendation. | Guarantees for unobserved architectures, models or online traffic; success from training accuracy |

For H3, log direction and targeted mediator **before** intervention data are acquired. H3q's predicted mediator direction is fixed; H3c/r/k require specific intervention magnitude and direction in an approved amendment after feasibility probes, without using held-out policy outcomes. They are prospective mechanism tests, not fully preregisterable numerical experiments yet. This incompleteness is explicit.

H2a and H2b answer different questions: a large interaction can leave the order unchanged; a sign change smaller than δ is not a practical reversal. C4 may justify transferring only a top-policy choice even where a complete ordering is unresolved; label those two claims separately.

There is no hypothesis that A100 is faster, TP2 beats DP2, SLAI wins, or reversals must occur. Stable rankings with narrow intervals are useful evidence. All-tied and low-precision rankings are not forced into a total order.
