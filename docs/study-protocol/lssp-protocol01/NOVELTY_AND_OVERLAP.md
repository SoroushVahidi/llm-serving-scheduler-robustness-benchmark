## Claims to remove from any future novelty statement

Do not claim first hardware comparison, first use of SLO goodput, first discovery of prefill/decode contention, first workload-dependent TP/DP/PP choice, first ranking portability study, or a new SLAI/VTC algorithm. Do not count native chunking and an equivalent renamed chunking policy as separate contributions. A credible submission needs the conjunction of C1–C4 evidence; the present document proposes it and establishes no empirical result.

## Runtime documentation used for design boundaries

The [vLLM 0.27.1 optimization documentation](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/v0.27.1/configuration/optimization/#chunked-prefill) describes decode-prioritized chunked prefill and recomputation under KV pressure. The [DP deployment documentation](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/v0.27.1/serving/data_parallel_deployment/) distinguishes native and external load balancing and per-rank sequence limits. These support the factor definitions, but do not validate our adapters or authorize removing their rejection checks. The main study should retain this pinned engine where the new contracts can be validated; a required version change must be uniform across policies, independently tested and recorded before freezing performance comparisons.
